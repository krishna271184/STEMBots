Curriculum

A research-backed curriculum, mapped to NEP 2020 and your timetable.

Built on the Carnegie Mellon Robotics Academy (CMRA) framework and accredited by STEM.org, the STEMBots curriculum runs from primary through upper secondary in standard 40–45 minute periods.

Foundation

Why CMRA and STEM.org

Rather than build a curriculum from scratch, STEMBots delivers CMRA's curriculum — research-backed, industry-endorsed, and pre-aligned to international STEM standards — under STEM.org accreditation, then maps every unit against NEP 2020's skill-education goals.

Progression

What's taught, stage by stage.

PRIMARY

Explore & build

  • Guided LEGO Spike Essential building and simple mechanisms
  • Introduction to sensors, motors and basic circuits
  • Virtual robot simulation as a low-risk first step
  • Storytelling-driven challenges to build early problem-solving habits
SECONDARY

Design & program

  • LEGO Spike Prime and Arduino-based build-and-code challenges
  • Structured electronics: breadboarding, components, real circuits
  • Introductory IoT projects connecting sensors to outcomes
  • Team-based problem statements that mirror competition formats
UPPER SECONDARY

Apply & compete

  • Applied AI/ML projects, from simple models to more complex applications
  • Robotics & mechatronics: servo motors, actuators, sensor integration
  • VEX and advanced Arduino builds for open-ended engineering problems
  • Direct preparation for FLL, RoboChampionship and FTC competition cycles
Delivery format

Built to fit inside the school day.

Each session runs 40–45 minutes — a standard class period — so skill education is scheduled like any other subject rather than bolted on as an extracurricular. Sessions blend hands-on building with concept instruction, and every unit closes with a practical build or demonstration.

  • Session length: 40–45 minutes, aligned to standard timetabling
  • Class range: primary through upper secondary
  • Format: physical hardware kits paired with virtual robot simulation
  • Assessment: practical build outcomes plus concept checks
TUTOR CERTIFICATION

CMRA Teacher Training

Every tutor delivering the STEMBots curriculum is trained and certified through Carnegie Mellon Robotics Academy's teacher training programme before leading a classroom.

  • Hands-on training on each lab's kits and software
  • Instruction in the CMRA pedagogical approach
  • Ongoing support and refresher certification
  • Preparation for coaching students toward competitions
