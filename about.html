About STEMBots

Founded on the same idea we teach: build it yourself, then help others build.

STEMBots was started by an engineer and robotics coach who has lived on both sides of the gap we now help schools close — the gap between what's studied in a classroom and what's actually built in the real world.

Founder's note
"Every research paper I've published and every robotics team I've coached has taught me the same lesson — students don't fall in love with a subject by reading about it, they fall in love with it by building something that works, watching it fail, and fixing it. STEMBots exists to give every school the lab, the curriculum and the trained tutor to make that possible." — Krishna Kumar Nagalingam, Founder

Why STEMBots exists

Three habits carried straight from the lab and the field into the classroom.

01

Learn by building, not by reading about it

Years of supervising research projects reinforced one thing: understanding deepens fastest through hands-on inquiry, iterative testing and honest feedback — the same cycle of Identify, Plan, Act and Measure applied in the classroom.

02

Competitions turn skill into confidence

Coaching FLL teams from a first regional win through to Scandinavian-level competition and the Open Africa Championship showed how a real competition deadline accelerates learning far faster than a classroom exercise alone.

03

Standards matter

A career built on peer-reviewed research and rigorous engineering assessment shaped STEMBots' insistence on accredited curriculum and certified tutors, rather than ad-hoc lab kits with no instructional backbone.
